Privacy Policy

Interplanetary Dub Portal · Arsenic Audio · Last updated 25 May 2026

Interplanetary Dub Portal is an audio plugin made by Arsenic Audio. We believe your privacy matters, so we built this thing to leave you completely alone.

What we collect

Nothing. The plugin does not collect, store, log, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or any other information about you or your computer.

Tracking and analytics

None. There is no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting service, no cookies (it is not that kind of software anyway). The plugin runs entirely on your machine and does not phone home.

Internet access

Interplanetary Dub Portal does not connect to the internet for any reason. It does not check for updates, it does not validate licences online, and it does not communicate with any server.

Third parties

We do not share information with any third party, because there is no information to share.

Children

Since we collect no data from anyone, this includes anyone under the age of 13. The app is rated 4+.

Changes to this policy

If anything ever changes in how the plugin handles data (we have no plans to change it), we will update this page and revise the date at the top.
